How this works

Cloud products are rented; mental models are owned. Every lesson cycles through seven beats — intuition before jargon, mechanism before tools, and a quiz so you can't lie to yourself.

MOTTOThe whole lesson in one sentence.
PROBLEMThe concrete pain that makes this topic exist.
CONCEPTAnalogies and ASCII diagrams before any jargon.
BUILD ITThe mechanism, from scratch — code included.
USE ITThe production tools, and their tradeoffs.
WAR STORYA real outage, paper, or engineering legend.
CHECKPOINTQuiz questions. Can't answer? Re-read.

About

Frameworks change. Fundamentals compound.

When you know why Kafka partitions exist, every message queue makes sense. When you've built consistent hashing by hand, every sharded database is familiar. This curriculum goes from the spinning disk to the multi-region deployment — 20 phases forming a dependency chain, ending in whiteboard case studies, interview mastery, and capstones you actually ship. MIT licensed. No paywalls. Runs on your laptop.
